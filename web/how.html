Design choices

  • Her words, not the model's. Every question cites the transcript lines it came from, and the answer screen replays those lines. A question without evidence is never asked until the family checks it.
  • Health waits for family. Anything about medicine, doses or symptoms is only asked after a family member checks it against what the doctor said. The model never adds advice.
  • Her language first. Questions, hints and answers are in Nepali with respectful forms; English is for the family and is always shown beside the original.
  • Private by default. Any conversation can be kept private: no questions, nothing shown. With real data the server requires an access token.
  • She keeps her word. A promise said out loud becomes a Bee reminder within seconds, with an Undo for the family. The evening digest links it instead of suggesting it twice.
  • Bee learns her world. Facts Bee knows ("Kamala is my sister") name the people in her conversations; new lasting facts are saved back to Bee only when the family approves.
  • Open source. Bee data comes through bee-cli-python, a Python client we built for every Bee CLI command, with typed live events and a fake Bee for tests.
  • Built on open standards. The evening screen is itself an MCP client, so any Alexa+ device or MCP host can run the same practice.
