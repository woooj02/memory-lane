# Product: Memory Lane (सम्झना)

Evening memory practice for older parents, built from their own day.

- The parent (first user: a Nepali-speaking mother in her 60s) records her day with Bee on an Apple Watch.
- Each evening the app asks about 6 short questions about her real day, in her language, in the order the day happened. She gets a hint if she's stuck, then the answer with the exact words that were said.
- Missed questions come back tomorrow; known ones come back after 2, 4, 7, then 14 days.
- Things she promised out loud become Bee todos with an alarm.
- The family page shows every line in Nepali beside its English, and how practice is going. Health questions wait for a family member's approval.

Rules that never bend:
- Every card must cite transcript (or Bee data) evidence. Never invent a detail.
- Health: only quote what was said. Never give advice.
- Warm, respectful language (तपाईं, not तिमी). Large type, light theme. Older eyes.
- It is practice, not treatment. Never claim it treats or prevents memory loss.

Built for the Amazon "Build, Ship, Shape" hackathon: Bee track (primary) and Alexa+ (MCP server).
